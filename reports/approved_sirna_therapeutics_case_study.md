# Approved siRNA therapeutics: clinical-translation case study

## Purpose and scope

This concise case study connects the project’s sequence-and-context workflow to publicly documented clinical applications of RNA interference (RNAi). It is a background resource for interview discussion, not a clinical comparison or a source of efficacy claims. Indications and labels can differ by jurisdiction and change over time; the descriptions below reflect publicly available U.S. regulatory materials accessed on 2026-09-28.

| Drug name | Target gene | Therapeutic area / indication | siRNA mechanism | Key delivery / development concept (publicly known) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Patisiran (Onpattro)** | `TTR` | Polyneuropathy of hereditary transthyretin-mediated amyloidosis (hATTR) in adults | A double-stranded siRNA targets a conserved region of wild-type and variant `TTR` mRNA; RNAi-mediated mRNA degradation reduces hepatic TTR protein production. | Lipid nanoparticle (LNP) formulation delivers siRNA to hepatocytes, the principal source of circulating TTR. |
| **Givosiran (Givlaari)** | `ALAS1` | Acute hepatic porphyria (AHP) in adults | A double-stranded siRNA drives RNAi-mediated degradation of hepatic `ALAS1` mRNA. | Covalent triantennary N-acetylgalactosamine (GalNAc) ligand supports hepatocyte delivery after subcutaneous administration. |
| **Lumasiran (Oxlumo)** | `HAO1` | Primary hyperoxaluria type 1 (PH1) | An `HAO1`-directed siRNA acts through RNAi in hepatocytes, reducing expression of glycolate oxidase. | GalNAc-conjugated siRNA illustrates receptor-mediated hepatocyte targeting for a metabolic disease context. |
| **Inclisiran (Leqvio)** | `PCSK9` | LDL-C lowering in adults with primary hyperlipidemia | A double-stranded siRNA directs RNAi-mediated breakdown of `PCSK9` mRNA in hepatocytes, reducing PCSK9 production. | Sense-strand triantennary GalNAc conjugation facilitates hepatocyte uptake; this case extends RNAi into a prevalent cardiometabolic indication. |
| **Vutrisiran (Amvuttra)** | `TTR` | hATTR polyneuropathy and wild-type or hereditary ATTR cardiomyopathy in adults under the current U.S. label | A double-stranded siRNA-GalNAc conjugate drives RNAi-mediated degradation of mutant and wild-type `TTR` mRNA. | GalNAc-conjugated, chemically modified siRNA supports hepatocyte targeting and shows how delivery/chemistry can evolve within the same biological target family. |

## What these cases demonstrate

These examples show the clinical value of RNAi therapeutics as a modality for reducing production of disease-relevant proteins by targeting their mRNA. They also make delivery a central computational and translational consideration: patisiran uses an LNP formulation, while the later examples above use hepatocyte-targeting GalNAc conjugation. The same RNAi principle is represented across rare genetic/metabolic diseases and a cardiometabolic indication.

For this project, the cases motivate why sequence alone is insufficient for siRNA analysis. Target identity, delivery strategy, chemical modification, assay context, and activity endpoint are all part of the interpretable development context. This document does not infer that any sequence feature in the project dataset explains clinical performance.
